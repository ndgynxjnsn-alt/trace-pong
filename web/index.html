Every ball is one call between two services, replayed from Tempo traces. The request flies to the callee, a paddle swings round to meet it, and the response goes back once the span and its own calls are done. Errors get past the paddle and explode. Rings show health (green ok / red errors), as in Grafana's service graph. Rates and latencies come from Tempo's service-graph metrics in Mimir.
